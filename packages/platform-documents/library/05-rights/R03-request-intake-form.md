# Your Privacy Rights Portal — [Client-Brand-Name]

**Intro text (portal landing):**
Use this page to see, correct or delete the personal data [Client-Brand-Name] holds about you, to nominate someone to act for you, to withdraw consent, or to raise a concern. This service is free. We will acknowledge your request within [Rights-Ack-Period] and respond within [Rights-Response-Period].

**Choose a request type**

| Option (button) | Help text |
|---|---|
| **See my data** | Get a summary of the personal data we hold about you, how we use it, and who we have shared it with. |
| **Correct or update my data** | Fix something that is wrong, incomplete or out of date. |
| **Delete my data** | Ask us to erase data we no longer need. Some data we must keep by law; we will tell you what and why. |
| **Withdraw consent** | Stop a particular use of your data (e.g. marketing). This is as easy as giving consent. You can also do it instantly at [Consent-Preferences-URL]. |
| **Nominate someone** | Choose a person who can exercise your rights if you die or are unable to. |
| **Raise a grievance** | Tell us about a concern with how we handle your data. |
