> **Drafting note:**
> 1. **Legal basis.** Section 7(i) lets an employer process personal data without consent "for the purposes of employment or those related to safeguarding the employer from loss or liability" (e.g. prevention of corporate espionage, confidentiality of trade secrets / IP / classified information) or to provide a service or benefit the employee seeks. Section 5 notice is strictly required only for consent-based processing. Even so, a workforce notice is strongly recommended for transparency, for s.13 grievances, and to evidence the scope of s.7(i).
> 2. **Candidates.** Whether pre-hire recruitment falls under "purposes of employment" is not settled. The conservative approach used here is to take **consent** for candidates, especially for background verification (BGV), talent-pool retention and reference checks, and to switch to s.7(i) once an offer is accepted.
> 3. **Rights.** Rights under s.11 (access) and s.12 (correction/erasure) attach to consent and s.7(a) processing. Many employers extend them to all employees as policy. The template does the same, with statutory-record carve-outs.
> 4. Render this as two parts (Part A Candidates, Part B Employees & Workforce), or as two separate documents.

# Workforce Privacy Notice

This notice explains how **[Client-Name]**<!-- IF Is-Group-Company --> and its group companies listed in Annexure 1<!-- END IF --> ("**we**", "**the Company**") process the personal data of job applicants, employees, interns, trainees, consultants, and contract and temporary workers ("**you**"), under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.

The Company is the **Data Fiduciary**. Where you are deployed through a staffing agency or contractor, that agency is a separate Data Fiduciary for your employment with it. We process only the data we need to manage your engagement with us.

## PART C — COMMON TO ALL

### C1. Security
We protect workforce data with role-based access (HR data is visible only to authorised HR, payroll and line-manager roles), encryption, logging and monitoring, vendor contracts and staff training (Rule 6).

### C2. Your rights
You can:
- **access** a summary of your personal data and the list of recipients (HRMS self-service: [HRMS-Self-Service-Path], or [HR-Privacy-Contact-Email]);
- **correct, complete or update** your data (most fields are editable in self-service);
- **request erasure** of data that is no longer needed. Statutory records (payroll, PF, tax, POSH, litigation) cannot be erased before the legal retention period ends;
- **withdraw consent** for consent-based purposes (A-P1 to A-P4, B-P11 to B-P13) at [Consent-Preferences-URL] or by emailing [HR-Privacy-Contact-Email]. Withdrawal does not affect any employment decision already lawfully made;
- **nominate** a person to exercise your data rights if you die or become incapacitated (Form D, R03). This nomination is separate from PF / gratuity / insurance nominations;
- **raise a grievance** with the Grievance Officer, and then complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Raising a privacy concern in good faith will never lead to adverse action against you.

### C3. Your duties
Please give accurate information, update it when it changes (address, bank, dependants), and do not give another person's data without their knowledge. Under section 15 of the Act, Data Principals must not impersonate others, suppress material information, or file false or frivolous grievances.
