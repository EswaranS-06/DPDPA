> **Drafting note:** Section 10(2)(b) requires an SDF to appoint an **independent data auditor** to evaluate its compliance with the Act. The Rules do not yet prescribe the auditor's qualifications (**VERIFY** whether any Board or MeitY guidance has since been issued). Good practice: a firm with no conflict (not the firm that implemented the programme, and therefore not [Server-name] if [Server-name] implemented it), with qualified privacy and IS auditors (e.g. CISA, CIPP/CIPM, ISO 27701 LA, CERT-In empanelment for technical testing).
