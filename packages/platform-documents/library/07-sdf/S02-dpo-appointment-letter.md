> **Drafting note:** Under s.10(2)(a), an SDF's DPO must: (i) represent the SDF under the Act; (ii) be **based in India**; (iii) be an **individual responsible to the Board of Directors** or a similar governing body; and (iv) be the **point of contact for the grievance redressal mechanism**. Avoid conflicts of interest: don't appoint someone who decides purposes and means of processing in a way that compromises independence (for example the head of marketing, or the CIO alone). For non-SDFs, use the same letter with the title "Privacy Officer / person designated under section 8(9)", and delete the SDF-only clauses.

## PART A: BOARD RESOLUTION
**Certified true copy of the resolution passed by the Board of Directors of [Client-Name] at its meeting held on [Board-Meeting-Date]**

"RESOLVED THAT, pursuant to section 10(2)(a) of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the rules made under it, **[DPO-Name]**, [DPO-Designation], who is based in India, be and is hereby appointed as the **Data Protection Officer** of the Company with effect from [DPO-Effective-Date];

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT the Data Protection Officer shall represent the Company under the said Act, shall be responsible to the Board of Directors, shall be the point of contact for the Company's grievance redressal mechanism, and shall have the authority, resources and independence set out in the appointment letter placed before the Board;

RESOLVED FURTHER THAT [Authorised-Director] be and is hereby authorised to issue the appointment letter, to publish the business contact information of the Data Protection Officer on the Company's website and mobile application, and to do all acts necessary to give effect to this resolution."

Certified by: [Company-Secretary-Name], Company Secretary · Date: [Resolution-Certified-Date]

## PART B: APPOINTMENT LETTER
[Client-Letterhead]
Date: [DPO-Letter-Date]
To: [DPO-Name], [DPO-Address]

**Subject: Appointment as Data Protection Officer**

Dear [DPO-Name],

Further to the resolution of the Board of Directors dated [Board-Meeting-Date], we are pleased to appoint you as the **Data Protection Officer ("DPO")** of [Client-Name] (the "Company") with effect from [DPO-Effective-Date], on the following terms.

**1. Statutory role.** You will:
(a) represent the Company under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("Act"), including before the Data Protection Board of India;
(b) be responsible to the Board of Directors, and report to it at least [DPO-Board-Reporting-Frequency];
(c) be the point of contact for the Company's grievance redressal mechanism;
(d) remain based in India throughout your appointment.

**2. Responsibilities.** You will:
- oversee the privacy programme and policies (G01–G06);
- maintain the RoPA;
- advise on DPIAs and lead the periodic DPIA (Rule 13(1));
- coordinate the independent data audit (S03), and the submission of significant observations to the Board (Rule 13(2));
- oversee rights requests and grievances;
- lead the privacy aspects of breach response and the intimations to the Board (Rule 7);
- oversee algorithmic due diligence (Rule 13(3)) and data-localisation compliance (Rule 13(4));
- run training and awareness;
- monitor processors;
- handle all correspondence with the Data Protection Board.

**3. Independence and resources.**
- You will not receive instructions on how to perform your statutory tasks.
- You will not be penalised for performing them.
- You will have direct access to the Board of Directors and to the Audit Committee.
- You will have access to all personal data and processing operations.
- You will have a budget of [DPO-Budget] and a team of [DPO-Team-Size].
- You will have the authority to stop processing that poses imminent serious risk, pending a review by the Privacy Steering Committee.

**4. Conflicts of interest.** You will not hold any other role in which you decide the purposes or means of processing personal data in a way that conflicts with these duties. Any other role you hold ([DPO-Other-Roles]) has been assessed as free of conflict.

**5. Confidentiality.** You will keep confidential all information you obtain in this role, subject to your duties to the Board and the law.

**6. Term and removal.** The appointment continues until [DPO-Term-End] / until revoked by the Board of Directors. Removal or replacement requires a Board resolution, and the published contact information will be updated within [Contact-Update-Days] days.

**7. Remuneration.** As per your employment terms dated [Employment-Terms-Date] / as set out in Annexure 1.

Please sign and return a copy to signify your acceptance.

For [Client-Name]
[Authorised-Director], [Authorised-Director-Designation]

**Acceptance:** I accept the appointment on the above terms and confirm that I am based in India.
[DPO-Name] · Signature ________ · Date ________
