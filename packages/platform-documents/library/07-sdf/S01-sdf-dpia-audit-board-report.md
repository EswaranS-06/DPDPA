> **Drafting note:** Rule 13(1) requires an SDF to carry out a DPIA **and** an audit **once in every 12 months**, counted from the date it was notified as an SDF or included in a notified class. Rule 13(2) requires the SDF to have **the person who carried out the DPIA and audit** furnish a report of **significant observations** to the Board. This template is that report. The full DPIA (G05) and the audit report (S03) are kept as annexures or supporting documents, and are produced if the Board asks for them.
