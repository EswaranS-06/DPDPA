# Section 12 - Right to correction and erasure of personal data

Chapter III: Rights and Duties of Data Principal | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Correction, completion, updating and erasure of data processed on consent / s.7(a); erasure unless retention necessary for specified purpose or law.
