# Section 11 - Right to access information about personal data

Chapter III: Rights and Duties of Data Principal | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Summary of personal data and processing; identities of Data Fiduciaries and Processors it was shared with + description of data shared. Applies to consent and s.7(a) processing.
