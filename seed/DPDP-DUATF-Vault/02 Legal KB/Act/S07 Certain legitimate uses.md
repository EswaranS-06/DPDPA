# Section 7 - Certain legitimate uses

Chapter II: Obligations of Data Fiduciary | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Closed list (a)-(i) of processing without consent: voluntary provision, State benefits, State functions, legal disclosure, court orders, medical emergency, epidemic, disaster/public order, employment.
