# Section 10 - Additional obligations of Significant Data Fiduciary

Chapter II: Obligations of Data Fiduciary | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Notified by Central Govt based on volume/sensitivity, risk to rights, sovereignty, electoral democracy, security, public order. DPO in India responsible to board, independent data auditor, periodic DPIA and audit, other measures.
