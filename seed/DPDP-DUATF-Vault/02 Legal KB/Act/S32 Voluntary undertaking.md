# Section 32 - Voluntary undertaking

Chapter VII: Appeal and Alternate Dispute Resolution | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Board may accept voluntary undertaking at any stage; bars proceedings on its contents unless breached.
