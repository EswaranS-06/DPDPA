# Section 9 - Processing of personal data of children

Chapter II: Obligations of Data Fiduciary | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Verifiable consent of parent / lawful guardian; no detrimental processing; no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising directed at children; exemptions via Fourth Schedule.

- [[OBL-CHD-04]] No detrimental processing
- [[OBL-CHD-05]] No tracking, behavioural monitoring, targeted ads
