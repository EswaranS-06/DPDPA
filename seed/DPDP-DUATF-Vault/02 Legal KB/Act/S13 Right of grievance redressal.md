# Section 13 - Right of grievance redressal

Chapter III: Rights and Duties of Data Principal | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Readily available means of grievance redressal from Data Fiduciary/Consent Manager; respond within prescribed period (90 days max per Rule 14(3)); exhaust before approaching Board.
