# Section 16 - Processing of personal data outside India

Chapter IV: Special Provisions | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Central Govt may restrict transfer to notified countries/territories (negative list). Sectoral laws providing higher protection/restriction on transfer continue to apply (16(2)).
