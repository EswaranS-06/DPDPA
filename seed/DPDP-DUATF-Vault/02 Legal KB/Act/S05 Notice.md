# Section 5 - Notice

Chapter II: Obligations of Data Fiduciary | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Every consent request accompanied/preceded by notice (data, purpose, how to withdraw/exercise rights, how to complain to Board). Notice for legacy consents given before commencement 'as soon as reasonably practicable'. English or Eighth Schedule language option.
