# Section 27 - Powers and functions of Board

Chapter VI: Powers, Functions and Procedure of Board | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Direct urgent remedial measures on breach, inquire on complaint/reference/breach intimation, impose penalty, (1)(d) inquire into Consent Manager breaches (Phase 2), refer for blocking.
