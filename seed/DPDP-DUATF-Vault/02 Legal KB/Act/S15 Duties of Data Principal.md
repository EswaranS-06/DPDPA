# Section 15 - Duties of Data Principal

Chapter III: Rights and Duties of Data Principal | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Comply with law, no impersonation, no suppression of material information, no false/frivolous complaints, furnish verifiably authentic information for correction. Penalty up to Rs 10,000.

## Atomic obligations
- [[OBL-RGT-08]] Handle requests considering Data Principal duties
