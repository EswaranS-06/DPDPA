# Section 3 - Application of Act

Chapter I: Preliminary | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Applies to digital personal data processed in India (collected digitally or digitised) and to processing outside India connected with offering goods/services to Data Principals in India. Excludes personal/domestic use and data made publicly available by the Data Principal or under legal obligation.

## Atomic obligations
- [[OBL-SCP-01]] Determine applicability of the Act
- [[OBL-MAP-02]] Identify digitised non-digital data
