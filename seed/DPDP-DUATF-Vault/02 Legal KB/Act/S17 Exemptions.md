# Section 17 - Exemptions

Chapter IV: Special Provisions | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
17(1)(a)-(f): legal claims, courts/regulators, crime, non-India principals under foreign contract, court-approved schemes, loan-defaulter assessment -> Ch II (except 8(1),8(5)), Ch III and s.16 disapplied. 17(2): notified State instrumentalities, research/archiving/statistics -> Act disapplied. 17(3): notified DFs incl. startups exempt from s.5, 8(3), 8(7), 10, 11. 17(4): State - 8(7), 12(3), 12(2) conditionally. 17(5): notified exemptions within 5 years.
