# Section 6 - Consent

Chapter II: Obligations of Data Fiduciary | **Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

## Summary
Consent: free, specific, informed, unconditional, unambiguous, clear affirmative action, limited to necessary data. Withdrawal with comparable ease; cease processing (and processors) within reasonable time. Consent Managers (6(7)-(9)); burden of proof on Data Fiduciary (6(10)). NB s.6(9) commences in Phase 2.

> Commencement split: s.6(1)-(8),(10) Phase 3; s.6(9) Phase 2
