# Second Schedule - Standards for State processing (s.7(b)) and research/archiving/statistics (s.17(2)(b))

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

Lawful processing, purpose limitation, necessity/minimisation, reasonable accuracy efforts, retention only as necessary/by law, reasonable security safeguards, intimation to DP (contact, means to exercise rights), accountability of person determining purpose.
