# Third Schedule - Retention periods under Rule 8(1)

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

E-commerce entity >= 2 crore registered users in India; online gaming intermediary >= 50 lakh; social media intermediary >= 2 crore: 3 years from last approach or commencement of Rules (later), for all purposes except user account access and virtual tokens (wallet/money/goods).
