# Fourth Schedule - Exemptions for children's data (s.9(1),(3))

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13)

Part A classes: clinical/mental health establishments & healthcare professionals (health services); allied healthcare professionals (treatment/referral); educational institutions (tracking/monitoring for education/safety); creche/child day care (safety); child transport (location tracking during travel). Part B purposes: exercise of powers in child's interest under law; subsidy/benefit/service/certificate/licence/permit; creation of email account for communication; real-time location for safety; prevent access to harmful information; confirm DP is not a child.
