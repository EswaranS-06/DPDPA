# Rule 9 - Contact information of person to answer questions about processing

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
Publish prominently on website/app and mention in every response to a communication for exercise of rights: DPO (if SDF) or person able to answer questions.
