# Rule 6 - Reasonable security safeguards

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
Minimum: (a) encryption/obfuscation/masking/virtual tokens; (b) access control; (c) logs, monitoring, review; (d) continuity/backups; (e) retain logs & personal data 1 year; (f) processor contract clauses; (g) TOMs.
