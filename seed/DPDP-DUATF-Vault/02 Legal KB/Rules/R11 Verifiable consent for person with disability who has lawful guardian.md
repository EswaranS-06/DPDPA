# Rule 11 - Verifiable consent for person with disability who has lawful guardian

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
Verify guardian appointed by court, designated authority (RPwD Act 2016 s.15) or local level committee (National Trust Act 1999 s.13).
