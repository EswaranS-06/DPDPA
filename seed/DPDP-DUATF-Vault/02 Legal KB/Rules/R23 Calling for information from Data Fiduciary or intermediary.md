# Rule 23 - Calling for information from Data Fiduciary or intermediary

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
(1) furnish information for Seventh Schedule purposes to authorised person within time; (2) may prohibit disclosure of the request where it may prejudice sovereignty/security.
