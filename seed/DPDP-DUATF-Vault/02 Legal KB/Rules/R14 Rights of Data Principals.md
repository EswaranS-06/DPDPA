# Rule 14 - Rights of Data Principals

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
(1) publish means & identifiers; (2) request to DF; (3) grievance response within period not exceeding 90 days, publish, implement TOMs; (4) nomination; (5) 'identifier' definition.
