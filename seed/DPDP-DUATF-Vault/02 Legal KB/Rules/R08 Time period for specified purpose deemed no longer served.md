# Rule 8 - Time period for specified purpose deemed no longer served

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
(1) Third Schedule classes: erase after period of inactivity; (2) 48-hour prior intimation; (3) retain personal data, traffic data and logs min. 1 year for Seventh Schedule purposes.
