# Rule 7 - Intimation of personal data breach

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
(1) to each affected DP without delay: description, consequences, mitigation, safety steps, contact. (2)(a) to Board without delay: description, nature, extent, timing, location, likely impact; (2)(b) within 72 h (or extended): updated details, circumstances, mitigation, cause findings, remedial measures, report on DP intimations.
