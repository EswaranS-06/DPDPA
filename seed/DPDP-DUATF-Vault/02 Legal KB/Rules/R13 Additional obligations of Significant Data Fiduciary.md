# Rule 13 - Additional obligations of Significant Data Fiduciary

**Phase 3** (not yet in force - 2027-05-13) | G.S.R. 846(E)

## Summary
(1) DPIA + audit every 12 months; (2) report of significant observations to Board; (3) algorithmic/technical due diligence; (4) localisation of data specified by Central Govt (committee) incl. traffic data.
