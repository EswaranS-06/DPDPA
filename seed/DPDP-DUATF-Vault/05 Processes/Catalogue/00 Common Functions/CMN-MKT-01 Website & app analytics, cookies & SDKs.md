# CMN-MKT-01 - Website & app analytics, cookies & SDKs

**Sector:** All sectors (common functions) | **Department:** Marketing

> **Assessor note:** Most common hidden cross-border & children tracking risk. Inventory every SDK/pixel.

## Typical activities (create one `processing_activity` per row that exists at the client)
1. Cookie consent banner
2. Analytics tracking
3. Ad pixels / retargeting
4. A/B testing
5. Crash & performance SDKs
