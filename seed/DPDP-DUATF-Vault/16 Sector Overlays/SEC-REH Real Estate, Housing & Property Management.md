# SEC-REH - Real Estate, Housing & Property Management

**Covers:** Developers, brokers, co-working, RWAs/housing societies, facility management, society apps

**Regulators:** State RERA authorities, Local police, CERT-In

## Sector laws that run alongside DPDP (s.38: DPDP is in addition; s.16(2): stricter transfer rules survive)
| Law / instrument | Why it matters for personal data |
|---|---|
| RERA 2016 | Allottee records, project disclosures |
| Local police tenant/domestic help verification orders | Tenant & staff verification |
| Registration Act 1908 | Property documents |

## Localisation / cross-border
Society apps often on global cloud.

## Retention anchors (use in Retention Rules; reconciles s.8(7) 'retention required by law')
| Record | Period | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Allottee & sale records | Per RERA / contract / tax | RERA | verify |

> Confidence `verify` means you must confirm the current text before citing it in a client deliverable.

## DPDP hotspots in this sector
- Visitor & domestic staff data via society apps
- Tenant KYC shared with police
- Site-visit leads sold between brokers
- CCTV in common areas

## Where assessments get stuck here
- RWAs are DFs but lack capacity - app vendor as processor
- Brokers' WhatsApp lead forwarding

See also [[Stuck-Point Playbook]].

## Typical data principals
Home buyer, Tenant, Resident, Domestic help, Visitor, Broker
