# SEC-MED - Media, Entertainment & OTT

**Covers:** OTT platforms, broadcasters, publishers, music streaming, events & ticketing, ad-tech

**Regulators:** MIB, MeitY, TRAI (broadcasting), CERT-In

## Sector laws that run alongside DPDP (s.38: DPDP is in addition; s.16(2): stricter transfer rules survive)
| Law / instrument | Why it matters for personal data |
|---|---|
| IT Rules 2021 Part III - Digital Media Ethics Code | Age classification (U, U/A 7+, 13+, 16+, A), parental controls, age verification for A content |
| Cable Television Networks (Regulation) Act 1995 | Subscriber management (broadcast) |
| TRAI tariff/QoS regulations (broadcast) | Subscriber data |

## Localisation / cross-border
Ad-tech and measurement often cross-border.

## Retention anchors (use in Retention Rules; reconciles s.8(7) 'retention required by law')
| Record | Period | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Subscriber records | Per contract/tax | Tax laws | verify |

> Confidence `verify` means you must confirm the current text before citing it in a client deliverable.

## DPDP hotspots in this sector
- Profiles for kids
- Viewing-history profiling & ad targeting
- Contests & reality-show participant data
- Ticketing & event CCTV/face entry

## Where assessments get stuck here
- Shared household accounts - whose consent?
- Ad-tech bid-stream sharing - hundreds of recipients

See also [[Stuck-Point Playbook]].

Subscriber, Viewer/child profile, Participant/contestant, Event attendee
