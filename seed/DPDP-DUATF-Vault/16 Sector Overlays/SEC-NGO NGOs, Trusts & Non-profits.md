# SEC-NGO - NGOs, Trusts & Non-profits

**Covers:** NGOs, trusts, foundations, section 8 companies, CSR implementing agencies, child-care institutions

**Regulators:** MHA (FCRA), Income Tax Department, State Child Protection authorities, Charity Commissioners

## Sector laws that run alongside DPDP (s.38: DPDP is in addition; s.16(2): stricter transfer rules survive)
| Law / instrument | Why it matters for personal data |
|---|---|
| FCRA 2010 & Rules | Foreign contribution & donor records |
| Income-tax (s.80G donor reporting - Form 10BD) | Donor PAN & donation reporting |
| Juvenile Justice Act 2015 s.74 | Prohibition on disclosure of identity of children |
| Companies Act CSR rules | Reporting to corporate funders |

## Localisation / cross-border
Foreign donors/partners - cross-border.

## Retention anchors (use in Retention Rules; reconciles s.8(7) 'retention required by law')
| Record | Period | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| FCRA accounts & records | Per FCRA Rules (commonly 6 years) | FCRA Rules | verify |
| Donor records | Per Income-tax & FCRA | Income-tax | verify |

> Confidence `verify` means you must confirm the current text before citing it in a client deliverable.

## DPDP hotspots in this sector
- Beneficiaries incl. children, persons with disabilities, patients - vulnerable
- Photos/stories of beneficiaries in fundraising
- Volunteers & field workers with paper forms
- Data sharing with CSR funders and Government

## Where assessments get stuck here
- Consent literacy in rural/vulnerable populations - oral/assisted consent records
- Excel/WhatsApp as primary systems

See also [[Stuck-Point Playbook]].

Beneficiary, Child beneficiary, Donor, Volunteer, Field worker
