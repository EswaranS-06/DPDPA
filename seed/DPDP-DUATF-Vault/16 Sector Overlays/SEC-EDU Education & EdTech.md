# SEC-EDU - Education & EdTech

**Covers:** Schools, colleges, universities, coaching institutes, edtech platforms, test-prep

**Regulators:** Ministry of Education, UGC, AICTE, CBSE/State Boards, NCPCR, CERT-In

## Sector laws that run alongside DPDP (s.38: DPDP is in addition; s.16(2): stricter transfer rules survive)
| Law / instrument | Why it matters for personal data |
|---|---|
| RTE Act 2009 | Admissions incl. 25% EWS quota data |
| UDISE+ / APAAR ID framework | Student registry & APAAR ID creation (parental consent) |
| POCSO Act 2012 | Mandatory reporting; child protection |
| Juvenile Justice Act 2015 | Confidentiality of children in need of care |
| DPDP Fourth Schedule Part A(3) | Educational institutions exempt from s.9(1),(3) ONLY for tracking/behavioural monitoring for educational activities or safety |

## Localisation / cross-border
No localisation; edtech apps often use foreign SaaS/analytics.

## Retention anchors (use in Retention Rules; reconciles s.8(7) 'retention required by law')
| Record | Period | Source | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admission & academic records | Permanent for transcripts/degrees; others per board/university rules | Board/University rules | verify |
| CCTV in schools | Per State/CBSE safety guidelines (commonly 15-30+ days) | CBSE circulars | verify |

> Confidence `verify` means you must confirm the current text before citing it in a client deliverable.

## DPDP hotspots in this sector
- Children = default population (verifiable parental consent)
- EdTech platforms are NOT 'educational institutions' - Fourth Schedule may not help
- Proctoring (face, screen, audio) = biometric & monitoring
- Ad-tech/analytics on children's apps (s.9(3))
- Student wellbeing/counselling data

## Where assessments get stuck here
- Parent verification at scale for existing student base
- Teachers using personal WhatsApp groups
- Alumni databases built on child-era data

See also [[Stuck-Point Playbook]].

## Typical data principals
Student (minor), Parent/guardian, Adult student, Teacher, Applicant, Alumni
