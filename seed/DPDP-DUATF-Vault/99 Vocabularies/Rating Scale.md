# Rating Scale

Control rating scale (0-4, NA).

| Score | Label | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Not implemented | No control exists |
| 1 | Initial | Ad hoc, undocumented, person-dependent |
| 2 | Defined | Documented but not consistently applied / no evidence |
| 3 | Implemented | Consistently applied with evidence for the period |
| 4 | Effective | Tested, monitored, metrics, improving |
| NA | Not applicable | Engine or exemption says not applicable - justification required |
