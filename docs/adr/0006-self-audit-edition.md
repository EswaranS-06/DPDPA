# ADR-0006: The self-audit edition (C19)

Date: 5 Oct 2026. Status: accepted by the build; ComplyX's legal review of the question mapping is pending.

ComplyX asked for a second edition of DUATF where the auditor runs the whole assessment alone. The auditor creates the client's departments, chooses each department's questions from the three ComplyX templates, records the answers and evidence, and tracks everything, without sending links for the client to fill in. They also asked for DUATF's own simple sign-in page instead of Keycloak, while keeping role-based access, so that people such as an "IT Head" can be given work and, when needed, a login.

| # | Decision | Why | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Two editions on two branches.** `self-audit` is built here. `multi-view` keeps the earlier multi-user edition (Keycloak, client portal, playbook, demo data). `main` becomes the self-audit edition once it is complete and verified, and stays the long-term-support line | The editions differ in sign-in, people and the question bank; one codebase with switches would carry both everywhere | Tests of features that exist only in `multi-view` are marked `deferredTo: multi-view` in `plan/plan.yaml`. The C19 tests cover their replacements |
| 2 | **The question bank is the three ComplyX templates** (TPL-001 Data Fiduciary, 70 questions; TPL-002 Internal data handlers, 14, and 60 since ADR-0007 took ComplyX's Track B; TPL-003 External data handlers, 58). The workbooks live in `seed/question-bank/source`. `pnpm questions:import` turns them into `templates.yaml` | ComplyX maintains the questions in Excel. One file read by the build keeps the release reproducible | TC-C19.1-01 fails if `templates.yaml` and the workbooks differ |
| 3 | **Each question is mapped to the knowledge base** in `kb-mapping.yaml`: title, domain, obligations, controls, scoring, outcomes, a note and gates | References, penalty tier, phase and applicability must come from the KB obligations, not from the template's own citation | The mapping was drafted with AI help and needs ComplyX's legal review. Each `note` records where the template's citation differs from the KB (for example A1.1 cites s.10 and Rule 13, which apply only to Significant Data Fiduciaries) |
| 4 | **Answer types**: Yes / Partial / No; maturity 0-4; single and multiple choice; text. Maturity 3-4 counts as compliant, 2 as a potential gap and 0-1 as a gap. Yes/No can be scored in reverse (B0.7) or recorded only (B0.8, B0.14, B0.15). Each choice option carries its own outcome | The templates use these types. Scoring has to say which answers are gaps | Recorded answers are kept and shown, but left out of the compliance posture (TC-C19.4-02) |
| 5 | **Departments choose their questions**, one at a time, by section or a whole template. An answer belongs to one question in one department of one cycle. The first cycle opens by itself | The same question (for example the TPL-002 module) is asked of several departments | Findings and risks are per department. After a cycle is completed, changes to a department's questions go to the next cycle |
| 6 | **Self-check instead of review.** The auditor ticks each answer as checked once its evidence has been looked at. A cycle completes only when every question is answered and checked | There is no second reviewer in a one-person audit, but the auditor still needs a record of what was verified | Changing an answer clears its tick |
| 7 | **Questions reconcile themselves.** A question can depend on the answer to another one (its gate). For example, A1.4 "No" (not a Significant Data Fiduciary) rules out A1.2 and A12.1-A12.5. A ruled-out question is marked Not applicable, with the reason. A question that applies cannot be marked Not applicable, and a ruled-out question cannot be answered | Contradictory answers make the posture wrong | Gates are read across all departments in the cycle. Changing the gate answer undoes the automatic Not applicable (TC-C19.6-01) |
| 8 | **DUATF's own sign-in page**: username and password. Passwords are hashed with scrypt (N=2^15). After 5 failures the account locks for 15 minutes. A new login gets a one-time password that must be changed at first sign-in. Sessions are stored server-side as token hashes | ComplyX asked for a simple sign-in page that is part of DUATF | There is no authenticator-app second factor in this edition. It is meant for the office network; put it behind a VPN or a reverse proxy with its own access control before exposing it more widely |
| 9 | **Role-based access stays.** Roles: Administrator, Senior auditor, Auditor, DPO, Department owner, Viewer. Only auditors answer and check. Only the Administrator and the Senior auditor add people and issue logins | ComplyX: "keep the RBAC" | `assessment.answer` is limited to the audit team. `user.invite` belongs to the Administrator and the Senior auditor. `risk.accept` belongs to the Senior auditor or the DPO |
| 10 | **People without logins.** The Administrator or the Senior auditor adds a person such as "IT Head" and can give them questions, evidence requests, remediation actions and controls to own. A login can be issued later and revoked at any time | The auditor tracks who owns what, whether or not that person ever signs in | A signed-in person sees only their own work. They can upload evidence for it, but never answer. Their files wait for the auditor's review, while the audit team's own uploads are accepted at once. Open requests are listed for the auditor on the Evidence page and under "needs attention" |
| 11 | **One setup command for both systems.** `setup.sh` (Linux, macOS) and `setup.ps1` (Windows) fetch the code and dependencies, then call `pnpm duatf`, which handles install, update, repair, services, database, knowledge base, account, build, start/stop, logs, backup and doctor | ComplyX installs and updates DUATF on their own machines | Services run either in Docker (`infra/docker-compose.self.yml`, project `duatf-self`) or as existing services named in `infra/.env`. The defaults are web port 53500 and API port 54500, so this edition can run beside `multi-view` (53000/54000) |
