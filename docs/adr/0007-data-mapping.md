# ADR-0007: Personal data inventory, data mapping and record of processing (C20)

Date: 6 Oct 2026. Status: accepted by the build; ComplyX's legal review of the categories, levels, new data elements and the Track B mapping is pending.

ComplyX asked for three things in the self-audit edition: the full Track B question set for internal data handlers, more data elements in the knowledge base with their classification table checked, and a data mapping page. Every department answers one dedicated question when it is added, "what personal data does this department handle?", with suggestions, editable at any time. The answers make a data flow (in text and as a diagram) and a detailed record of processing (RoPA) that can be exported.

| # | Decision | Why | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **TPL-002 is ComplyX's full Track B** (TPL-005 export, 59 questions: the common module B0 and department modules B1 HR to B10 Facilities). Its question IDs match the 14 already in the bank | One internal handler questionnaire, not two overlapping ones | 187 questions in all. Codes such as B0.3a keep their spelling and match in any case |
| 2 | **B0.1 offers the data map's categories.** The mapping can replace a choice question's options (`options:` in `kb-mapping.yaml`) | The workbook lists industry sectors as the "categories of personal data" | TC-C19.1-02 checks that B0.1's options are the data map categories |
| 3 | **Categories and levels are DUATF's working classification, not the law.** 22 categories (ComplyX's 16, corrected, plus contact details, family, education, images and audio, consent records and other) and four levels, L1 Public to L4 Restricted. An element tagged financial, health, biometric or government ID is L4 | The DPDP Act has no special categories; the SPDI Rules 2011 do until 13 May 2027. ComplyX's table listed age and gender in three places, blood group as biometric, email as location data, a vendor's contact person as business data and payment details as a government identifier | Both are in the knowledge base as vocabularies (`personal-data-categories`, `data-classification-levels`), drafted for legal review. The app's copy lives in `packages/feature-compliance-api/src/personalData.ts` and the vocabularies are generated from it |
| 4 | **77 more data elements** (now 182), including demographic (DEM), authentication (AUT) and business identifier (BUS) families. Company identifiers are marked not personal data | Departments need to find what they hold without inventing names | Added through `kb-content`, labelled AI-drafted and awaiting review, like C17.4 |
| 5 | **The answer is per department and per element**: element, category, level, where it comes from (a group of people, another department or elsewhere), storage, security and access; plus the department's purposes, lawful basis, systems, departments it shares with, recipients outside, transfers outside India and retention | ComplyX's own working sheets list data, classification, level, from, storage, security and access per department | Stored in `department_data_element` and `department_data_profile`, under row-level security like every client table |
| 6 | **Suggestions come from the department's name**: ten department presets (HR, finance, procurement, IT, marketing, sales, support, admin, legal, product) and the knowledge base's process catalogue for the common functions and the client's sector | Fast to answer, and the same elements across clients | The auditor ticks what applies; nothing is added without a tick |
| 7 | **The data map is derived, never stored**: flows from people to departments, between departments, to recipients and outside India, with the highest level each carries. The RoPA is one row per department | One source of truth: change a department's answer and the map changes | The RoPA workbook has About, RoPA, Data inventory, Data flows and Categories sheets (TC-C20.4-01) |
| 8 | **Knowledge-base updates reach existing installations.** `pnpm duatf update` runs the KB step: drafted entries the release lacks are added and published, and a changed question bank builds a new release | Installations set up before C20 would never see the new questions or elements | A KB editor draft that is already open is never published by the setup |
